Tell us what it's about, then answer questions tailored to it
A quick domain check first, then the assessment adapts, focusing on one specific problem you're facing right now, not your whole life.
02
See it broken into 8 dimensions
Frequency, intensity, duration, scope, resources, urgency, complexity, and your own confidence, scored separately, then combined, so your score stays comparable no matter what the problem is.
03
Get a plan, not a lecture
A short list of real next actions, one exercise worth doing, and a prompt you can hand straight to an AI assistant.
Clarity OS is a structured self-reflection and problem-planning tool built around an original framework (APSI). It isn't therapy, medical advice, or a diagnosis. If what you're facing involves your safety or someone else's, please also talk to a professional.
