2. Hosting

The website is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you open a page, your browser sends your IP address and standard request details (time, page, browser type) to GitHub, which needs them to deliver the page and may keep them briefly in server logs for security. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our interest in running a secure website. GitHub is covered by the EU US Data Privacy Framework.

3. Your answers stay in your browser

The assessment runs entirely in your browser. Your answers, your result and your exercise notes are saved in your browser's local storage on this device only, so you can come back to your result. This includes:

  • your answers and your result (to resume later)
  • everything you type into the exercises
  • your light or dark theme choice
  • a random ID, created only if you choose to share (see section 4)

None of this is sent anywhere unless you press "Share with Kishan". Your exercise notes are never sent. You can delete all of it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

4. Optional sharing with Kishan

At the end you can choose to share your result to help test and improve Clarity OS. If you press "Share with Kishan", exactly this is sent:

  • your total score and severity band
  • your 8 dimension scores
  • your raw answers (1 to 5) to the 12 scored questions (q1 to q12)
  • your gut check number (0 to 100)
  • the area of life you picked (for example "work" or "money")
  • your approach style label (for example "structured"), worked out from two questions; the answers themselves are not sent
  • data quality signals: how long your answers took (median and total time, and how many answers took under one second) and whether every answer was the same
  • a random anonymous ID stored in your browser, so a later retake from the same browser can be linked to your earlier result
  • a random ID for the submission itself and the time you shared
  • your three feedback answers and the optional note, if you wrote one

Not sent: your name or email (we never ask for them), your answers to the two follow up questions about your area of life, your exercise notes, or anything else you typed in the exercises. Please do not put names or other personal details into the feedback note.

Where it goes: a database run by Kishan on Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA), acting as our processor under a data processing agreement. Transfers to the USA are protected by the EU standard contractual clauses. The database only accepts new entries from this website; no visitor can read anyone's data. Like any web request, your IP address reaches Supabase's servers, but it is not saved with your answers.

Notification email: each time someone shares, Kishan receives a short email with the score, severity band, area of life and whether a note was written. The note itself is not included. This email is sent through Resend (Resend Inc., USA), acting as our processor.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, given by pressing the share button. Sharing is entirely optional and the tool works the same without it.

How long: shared entries are kept until the test phase ends, and deleted at the latest 24 months after you shared them.

Withdrawing consent: because we never collect your name or email, we usually cannot tell which entry is yours. If you want your entry deleted, email us the date and approximate time you shared and your score, and we will find and delete it.

5. Visitor counts (GoatCounter)

To count visits, we use GoatCounter (goatcounter.com), a privacy friendly, open source analytics service. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It records the page visited, the referring site, browser and screen size, and a country worked out from your IP address. Your IP address itself is not stored. We also count four anonymous events: a page view, starting the assessment, reaching the results and sending a share. No answers or scores are sent to GoatCounter. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our interest in knowing whether the tool is used.
